The Teacher's Journey

STEP 01

Secure Login

Log in through our secure portal to begin. Your data is fully encrypted and synced across all your devices in real-time.

STEP 02

Interactive Dashboard

Effortlessly manage your subjects, track real-time attendance logs, and organize student rosters from one intuitive, centralized hub.

STEP 03

Course Management

Setting up a new course is effortless. Just give it a name, and AttenZa automatically configures everything you need to start tracking attendance.

FaceID Attendance
